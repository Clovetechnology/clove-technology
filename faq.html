FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before you enroll.

Find quick answers about courses, eligibility, projects, certificates, admissions and learning at Clove Technology.

Who can join Clove Technology courses?

Our programs are designed for beginners, students, graduates and learners based on the eligibility listed for each course.

Do I need programming experience?

Not for Digital & AI Starter. Python and advanced technology programs may recommend basic computer or programming knowledge as described on the Courses page.

What courses are available?

Current programs include Digital & AI Starter, Python Career Builder, AI & Technology Program, and Aptitude & Placement Preparation.

Will I work on projects?

Yes. Courses include practical activities and projects appropriate to the learning level. Project details are listed on the Courses page.

Do you provide certificates?

Clove Technology plans to issue a Certificate of Completion when the academy's stated course requirements are met. Certificate verification is being developed as part of the student platform.

How can I know the course fee?

Course fees are currently provided by Admissions. Send a Student Enquiry to receive the current fee, batch schedule and admission information.

Can I attend a demo class?

Demo availability can vary by batch. Contact Admissions through the Student Enquiry page to ask about the current demo schedule.

Do you provide placement guarantees?

We focus on career preparation such as aptitude, projects, resumes, portfolios and interview readiness. We do not describe these services as a guaranteed job or placement.

How do I apply?

Use the Student Enquiry page, select your course and submit your details. The admissions team can then provide the next steps.

Will there be online learning?

Online learning options can be introduced based on the course and batch. Contact Admissions for the current delivery format.
